# True Access — "Hey Siri, describe this" (free, built into your iPhone)

You don't need to open the app or tap anything. Set this up once (about 3 minutes), then just say **"Hey Siri, describe this"** any time.

## What it does

The shortcut opens True Access straight into the describe flow — the same as opening this link:
`https://pure-photography.github.io/true-access/?action=describe&autostart=1`

## Setup (do once)

1. Open the **Shortcuts** app on your iPhone (it's built in — no download).
2. Tap the **+** (plus) at the top to make a new shortcut.
3. Tap **Add Action**, search for **"Open URLs"**, and tap it.
4. In the URL field, type exactly:
   `https://pure-photography.github.io/true-access/?action=describe&autostart=1`
5. Tap the shortcut's name at the top and rename it to **"Describe this"** (this is the phrase Siri will listen for).
6. Tap **Done**.

That's it. Now say **"Hey Siri, describe this"** — Siri opens True Access and it starts describing what's playing.

## Other shortcuts you can make the same way

To make each one: same steps as above, just a different name and URL.

## Even faster: Back Tap (no voice needed)

If you'd rather tap the back of your phone than talk:

1. iPhone **Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap**.
2. Choose **Double Tap** (or Triple Tap).
3. Scroll to **Shortcuts** and pick your **"Describe this"** shortcut.

Now double-tapping the back of your iPhone triggers a description — silent, hands-free, works with the screen off in your pocket (the app still needs to be the thing that opens, so keep your phone nearby).

## Honest notes

- **First run needs one tap.** Apple only lets a web page use the microphone after you've tapped something. The first time the shortcut runs, you'll land on a consent card with one big "Yes, allow the microphone" button — tap it once and you're set. After that, your phone remembers.
- **Keep True Access open while watching.** The shortcut opens the app fresh each time, which is fine — describing takes a few seconds either way.
- **The in-app voice trigger** ("describe this" said out loud, in the Hands-free card) stays on while you're watching, so you usually won't even need the shortcut mid-movie — it's mostly for starting the session.
- Everything here is free and built into iOS. No downloads, no accounts, no credits.
